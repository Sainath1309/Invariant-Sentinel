Each feature predicted from at most three others. Fit is R² on calibration data: 100% is perfect. Rules below 50% are too weak to trust and are not used.

    Which faults can be told apart

    Similarity of the fingerprint each feature's fault would leave. Near 1.0 means the system cannot tell the two apart and will say so. Known before any monitoring.

    Thresholds and where they come from

    Calibrated values are learned from healthy batches; heuristics are fixed choices, disclosed here.

    Sentinel versus a standard drift check

    Every situation is simulated on 30 different batches. The standard check uses the same calibrated limits, so any difference comes from the relationships, not from better thresholds. Ranges are 95% confidence intervals.

    Datasets

    Any CSV works if it has a numeric column to predict and at least three numeric columns that are related to each other.
